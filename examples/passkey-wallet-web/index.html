      immediately — no waiting, and without needing you. Spending stops for two

        Because who guards your account is never written on chain, your guardians
        can only step in if they hold a small invite from you. Save it and send a
        copy to each guardian — by message, email, however you reach them. Without
        it they cannot prove they are a guardian, even when they are willing to
        help. Keep a copy for yourself too.
